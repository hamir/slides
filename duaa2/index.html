أَدْعِيَة

Duas, phrase by phrase

25 short supplications from the Qurʾān and Sunnah. Each one is split into phrases, with the meaning under the Arabic and a short note on where it comes from.

Copy puts a dua on your clipboard as alternating Arabic and English lines, ready to paste into a message. Translations are phrase-level renderings meant for learning, so the wording follows the Arabic phrase order. The commentary notes summarise the hadith narrations and classical tafsīr around each dua.
